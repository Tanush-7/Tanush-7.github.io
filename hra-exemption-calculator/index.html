HRA Exemption Calculator

Paying rent and getting HRA? Work out how much of it can be exempt if you opt for the old tax regime.

Before you start

HRA exemption is only available if you opt for the old tax regime - the default new regime does not allow it. This tool does the standard least-of-three arithmetic; your employer applies it while deducting TDS, and the Income Tax portal is the final word. Enter annual figures. For anything case-specific (paying rent to family, shared flats, mid-year moves), confirm with your employer or a CA.

The 50% city list was expanded from 4 to 8 cities (adding Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad) by the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (Notification 22/2026, effective for tax year 2026-27 onward).

How the exemption is computed
RuleMeaning
The least of three(a) actual HRA received, (b) rent paid minus 10% of Basic+DA, (c) 50% of Basic+DA for the 8 listed cities, else 40%. The smallest of the three is exempt.
Old regime onlyIf you file under the default (new) regime, HRA exemption does not apply - compare both regimes before choosing.
ProofEmployers ask for rent receipts or the rental agreement. For annual rent above Rs 1 lakh, employers generally require the landlord's PAN (or a declaration if the landlord has none). The current evidence form (Form 124 under the 2026 Rules) also asks whether you are related to the landlord.
Rent to familyPaying rent to parents can be legitimate when there is a genuine agreement, actual bank transfers, and the parent reports it as income. Artificial arrangements can be rejected - get advice for your situation.
