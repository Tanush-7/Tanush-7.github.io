# Retirement Corpus Calculator (India)

Estimate the corpus you need at retirement and the monthly SIP that gets you there, in INR.

- **Problem source**: retirement-planning questions such as [WHEN CAN I PLAN MY RETIREMENT](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinanceindia/comments/1xr0zwp/) on r/personalfinanceindia.
- **Features**: inflation-adjusted expense at retirement, growing-annuity corpus target, existing-savings growth, step-up SIP solver, milestone table, zero-inflation/zero-return boundary handling, explicit assumptions.
- **Run it**: open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site.
- **Limits**: a projection from your inputs, not a prediction or advice; no rate is guaranteed. Consider a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
