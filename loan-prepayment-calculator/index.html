Before you start

This is an illustration using your numbers, not advice. Whether an extra payment is treated as part-prepayment of principal or as advance EMI depends on your lender and how the payment is marked - confirm the treatment in writing with your bank before paying, and check for prepayment charges in your loan agreement. Math here assumes a fixed rate, monthly reducing balance, and no fees.
