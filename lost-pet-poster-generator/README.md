# Lost Pet Poster Generator

Fill a short form, get a print-ready A4 lost-pet poster.

- **Problem source**: lost and abandoned pet posts such as [LOST PERSIAN CAT - near Indiranagar Club](https://www.reddit.com/r/bangalore/comments/1xqb4fd/) on r/bangalore.
- **Features**: photo from your device (never uploaded), name, breed, description, last-seen place and date, contact, reward line, live A4 preview, print/save-as-PDF stylesheet.
- **Run it**: open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site.
- **Privacy**: everything stays in the browser; the demo starts blank - use your own photo and details.
