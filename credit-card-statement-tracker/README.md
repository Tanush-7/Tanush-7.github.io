# Credit Card Statement Cycle Tracker

Free, single-file browser tool. Track credit-card spends against the real statement
cycle (e.g. 20th of one month to 19th of the next) instead of the calendar month,
so the statement total never surprises you.

- Set your statement close day and due-date offset (from your actual statement)
- Add spends/refunds; they are grouped into statement cycles automatically
- Shows current cycle total, days left, and estimated payment due date
- CSV export; all data stays in the browser's local storage

Not affiliated with any bank or card issuer. The issuer's own statement is the
final word on what you owe and when.
