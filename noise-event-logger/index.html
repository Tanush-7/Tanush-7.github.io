Noise Event Logger

Timestamped log of loud-noise events, kept on your device, exportable as CSV or a printable report.

What this is (and is not)

This page uses your microphone to note when loud sounds happen and roughly how loud they were relative to your room's baseline. Phone microphones are not calibrated, so the levels here are indicative, not measurements of any legal standard. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere - only relative loudness numbers are computed in your browser.

This tool only keeps a personal timestamped record. If you want to report a noise problem, the right channel depends on your city and situation - your local police station or your state police department's official app or online grievance portal are the usual starting points. 112 is an emergency number, not a routine noise-complaint line.
