Training plan

The 4-week body-recomposition block behind the fitness goal: 26 Oct – 22 Nov 2026. Morning sessions, 60–90 minutes. Tap a day to see the session.

Block rules

  • Effort: strength at RPE 7–8 (2–3 reps in reserve). Zone 2 at a pace where full sentences are possible.
  • Running rule: run only at pain 0–2/10, with no change in gait and no worse morning after. At 3+/10, sharp, or worsening — swap running for incline walking.
  • Fueling: eat before strength and run days. Fasted only for Thursday/Saturday Zone 2 and the Sunday walk.
  • Daily targets: 2,000–2,100 kcal · 150–160 g protein · 8,000–12,000 steps.
  • Weeks 2–4: run intervals lengthen (2 min → 3/2 → 4/2), an extra set on the big lifts in week 3, Zone 2 builds to 45–50 min, then a deload week with plank retests.
