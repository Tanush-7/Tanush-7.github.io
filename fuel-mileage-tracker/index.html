Fuel Mileage & Cost-per-KM Tracker

Log each refill with the odometer reading. The full-tank-to-full-tank method then gives you real km/l, cost per km, and monthly fuel spend.

How to get accurate numbers

Fill the tank to full (auto-cutoff), note the odometer, and drive normally. At the next full refill, note the odometer again and the litres filled - the litres of that second fill are what the distance between the two readings consumed. The first entry only sets the baseline, so mileage appears from your second fill onwards. Everything is saved only in this browser.
