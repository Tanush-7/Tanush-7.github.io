Train Cancellation Refund Calculator

"If I cancel now, how much do I get back?" Indian Railways moved to the 72 / 24 / 8-hour windows from April 2026 - see what the current rules give you before you hit cancel.

Current rules at a glance (confirmed tickets, regular trains)
When you cancel (before departure)Deduction
More than 72 hoursFlat charge per passenger by class: AC First/Executive Rs 240, First/AC 2-tier Rs 200, AC 3-tier/Chair Car/3 Economy Rs 180, Sleeper Rs 120, Second class Rs 60 (GST extra on AC classes)
24 to 72 hours25% of fare (never less than the flat charge above)
8 to 24 hours50% of fare (never less than the flat charge)
Under 8 hoursNo refund

These windows were revised from the old 48/12/4-hour ones, aligned with reservation charts now being prepared 9-18 hours before departure. The exact deduction appears on the IRCTC/app cancellation screen before you confirm - treat that screen as final, and check IRCTC's current refund compilation for your exact case (Tatkal, premium trains and concessions have their own treatments not modelled here).

Your ticket
Good to know
RAC / waitlistedCancel up to 30 minutes before departure: refund minus clerkage of Rs 60 per passenger (GST extra on AC classes). After that, no refund. Fully waitlisted e-tickets that stay unconfirmed at charting are cancelled automatically and refunded minus clerkage - you cannot travel on one.
E-ticket refundsSince the April 2026 changes, refunds are processed automatically on cancellation - the old requirement to file an online TDR for ordinary cancellations is removed.
Train cancelled by RailwaysFull refund automatically for e-tickets; counter tickets can be cancelled at any station (not just the origin) within the allowed window.
Train over 3 hours lateIf you do not travel, full fare back - but for e-tickets file the TDR before the train's actual departure.
