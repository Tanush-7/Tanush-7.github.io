Gratuity Calculator (India)

Leaving a job after a few years and wondering what gratuity adds up to? Run the statutory formula here.

Before you start

Gratuity in India is now governed by the Code on Social Security, 2020 (in force since 21 Nov 2025; it replaced the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972). This tool applies Section 53 of the Code: for a monthly-rated employee, gratuity is (last drawn monthly wages / 26) x 15 x years of service. What is actually payable still depends on your contract, your employer, and - in a dispute - the competent authority. Verify current figures and your case with your employer or the labour authorities.

"Wages" here means basic pay + dearness allowance + retaining allowance, if any. Under the Code's uniform definition (Section 2(88)), if your other allowances together exceed 50% of total remuneration, the excess is added back into wages - so for many salary structures the gratuity wage base is higher than just "basic". Use your payslip, and ask HR which figure they use.

The rules this calculator applies (Section 53, Code on Social Security 2020)
RuleWhat the Code says
Formula15 days' wages for every completed year of service (or part exceeding 6 months). For monthly-rated employees: (monthly wages last drawn / 26) x 15, per year. The 26 represents working days in a month.
RoundingService in excess of 6 months counts as a full year (6 years 7 months counts as 7). Exactly 6 months does not round up.
Eligibility5 years of continuous service, payable on superannuation, retirement or resignation. The 5 years are NOT required on death, disablement, or expiry of a fixed-term contract. Fixed-term employees become eligible after 1 year of service, paid pro-rata.
Continuous serviceThe Code counts a year as continuous if you actually worked at least 240 days in the 12 months (190 days for mines and <6-day-week establishments) - this is the statutory basis of the "4 years 240 days" idea you may have seen.
CapMaximum gratuity is as notified by the Central Government - Rs 20 lakh per the Ministry of Labour FAQ (as on 25 Oct 2026). Amounts above the cap are not statutory gratuity, though contracts can give better terms.
TaxPart of gratuity can be tax-exempt up to limits notified under the Income-tax Act 2025. Check the current limit on incometax.gov.in - this tool computes the gross amount only.
