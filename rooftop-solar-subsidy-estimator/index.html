The subsidy structure (official)

Under PM - Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential households get Central Financial Assistance of Rs 30,000 per kW up to 2 kW, plus Rs 18,000 per kW for additional capacity up to 3 kW - so Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW, Rs 78,000 for 3 kW, and capped at Rs 78,000 for anything above 3 kW. Group housing societies / RWAs get Rs 18,000 per kW for common facilities up to 500 kW. Applications go through the national portal pmsuryaghar.gov.in only, with a DISCOM-empanelled vendor; the subsidy is paid after installation and inspection. Verify the current structure on the portal before signing - scheme terms are revised from time to time.
