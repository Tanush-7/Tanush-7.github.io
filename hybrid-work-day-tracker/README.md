# Hybrid Work Day Tracker

Free, single-file browser tool for hybrid workers. Tap days on a calendar to mark
Office / WFH / Leave, and get monthly and year-to-date counts (including office
share) so you always know where you stand against your team's attendance policy.

- Month calendar, one tap cycles Office -> WFH -> Leave -> clear
- Monthly summary and year-to-date counts
- CSV export; all data stays in the browser's local storage

Company attendance policies vary - compare counts against the policy your HR
gave you.
