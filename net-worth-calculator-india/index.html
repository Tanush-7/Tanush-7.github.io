Net Worth Snapshot Calculator

Everything you own minus everything you owe. Add items with rough current values and see your net worth in one number.

Before you start

Use what each asset would fetch if you sold it today, not what you paid or hope it will be worth. This is a snapshot for your own clarity, not a valuation for tax, loan or legal purposes - those need documents and, where relevant, a professional. Nothing you type leaves this browser (local storage only).
