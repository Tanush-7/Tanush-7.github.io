# After a Death in the Family (India)

An ordered, printable checklist of the formalities families commonly face after a death.

- **Problem source**: [Reddit request (40 upvotes)](https://www.reddit.com/r/bangalore/comments/10lll07/what_are_some_problems_in_india_that_you_want_a/) — on a death, the pile of formalities: death certificate, succession, closing bank accounts, closing loans, insurance.
- **What it does**: phases from the first days (medical certificate, registration, certified copies) through banks, insurance, legal/tax, and closing loops; ticks are saved locally; print-friendly. Deep-links official sources (Civil Registration System, RBI deceased-depositor guidance, income tax portal, ECI).
- **Run it**: open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site.

General guidance, not legal advice. Exact documents vary by bank, insurer and state.
