HSN & GST Rate Verification Assistant

Decode an HSN code, get to the right official source, and keep a worksheet of the rates you verified yourself.

Why no ready-made rate table?

GST rates changed substantially with effect from 22 September 2025: the rationalisation to a two-rate structure (18% standard, 5% merit, 40% de-merit for select goods), per the GST Council's official press release, notified through Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17.09.2025, published in the Gazette of India (extraordinary) and superseding the 2017 rate notification (No. 01/2017-Central Tax (Rate)). Many rates are conditional - for example, the rate on apparel can depend on the sale value per piece, and food items can depend on whether they are branded or pre-packaged. A static table would go stale or mislead, so this tool deliberately does not assert any rate. It helps you classify the code and verify the rate from the official sources below, for your specific product and date.

Verify the rate at the official sources
  1. GST portal - Search HSN: the official taxpayer portal has a "Search HSN / SAC" service (no login needed) that shows the description and integrated tax rate for a code. Entry point: services.gst.gov.in → User Services → Search HSN Code. A walkthrough of the feature is on the official tutorial site: tutorial.gst.gov.in - Search HSN/SAC Tax Rates.
  2. Rate notifications: the legal text of every rate, exemption, and condition is in CBIC notifications, searchable on the CBIC Tax Information Portal (under GST → Notifications). The current master rate notification is No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17.09.2025, effective 22.09.2025 (Gazette copy), as amended - always check for later amendments for your product.
  3. Council decisions: rate-change background appears in GST Council press releases; the notification text is what actually applies.

Watch for conditions in the notification's schedule entries (value thresholds, branding, packaging, use). If two entries seem to cover your product, the more specific description and any chapter notes decide - when in doubt, a chartered accountant or an advance ruling is the reliable route.
