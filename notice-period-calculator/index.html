Notice Period & Last Working Day Calculator

Resigning and not sure when your last working day falls, or how many days you still owe? Do the date math here.

Before you start

This is date arithmetic only. Your actual notice terms - length, whether it counts calendar or working days, buyout options, leave adjustment, early relief - come from your appointment letter and your employer's policies, and HR's written confirmation is what counts. If salary is being withheld or there is a dispute, that is a separate issue; check your contract and, if needed, get advice specific to your situation.

How the counting works
TermMeaning
Calendar daysEvery day counts, including weekends and holidays. A 30-day notice given on 1 Nov ends on 1 Dec.
Working daysOnly Monday-Friday count (this tool does not know your company holidays). Less common - use only if your letter says so.
Buyout / recoverySome employers let you pay for unserved days or recover pay for them. Whether and how much is entirely company policy - ask HR in writing.
Leave adjustmentSome employers allow adjusting earned leave against notice; many do not. Only enter a number here if HR has agreed to it.
