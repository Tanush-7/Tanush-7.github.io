Notice Period & Last Working Day Calculator

Resigning and not sure when your last working day falls, or how much notice is left? Do the date math here.

Before you start

This is date arithmetic only. Your actual notice terms - length, whether it counts calendar or working days, buyout options, leave adjustment, early relief - come from your appointment letter and your employer's policies, and HR's written confirmation is what counts. If salary is being withheld or there is a dispute, that is a separate issue; check your contract and, if needed, get advice specific to your situation.

How the counting works
TermMeaning
Counting convention used hereThe submission day is day 0 and the last day is the notice length added to it: a 30-day notice submitted on 1 Nov ends on 1 Dec. If your employer counts the submission day itself as day 1, dates shift by one day - HR's written confirmation wins.
Calendar daysEvery day counts, including weekends and holidays.
Working daysOnly Monday-Friday count (this tool does not know your company holidays). Use this only if your appointment letter counts the notice in working days.
Days servedDays since your resignation date already sit inside the notice window, so they never move the last day earlier - they only tell you how much notice is left.
Leave adjustmentSome employers allow adjusting earned leave against notice; many do not. Only enter a number here if HR has agreed to it in writing - that is the only thing this tool lets move your last day earlier.
Buyout / recoverySome employers let you pay for unserved days or recover pay for them. Whether and how much is entirely company policy - ask HR in writing.
