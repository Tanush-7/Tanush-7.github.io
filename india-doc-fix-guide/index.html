Before you start (applies to most corrections)
  • Keep the original document number and a registered mobile number handy - most portals verify by OTP.
  • Names and dates of birth should match across Aadhaar, PAN and your bank records. Fix mismatches one document at a time; which one to start with depends on the correction you need - the official portal for that document lists what must match.
  • Use only official government portals. Agents charging "express" fees outside the portal are a common scam pattern.
Built after Reddit requests to make issuing/correcting government documents easier - source thread. General guidance, not legal advice. Procedures, fees and timelines change; the linked official portals are the source of truth.
