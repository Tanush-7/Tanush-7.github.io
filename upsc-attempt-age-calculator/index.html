UPSC Attempt & Age Calculator

"Do I have 2 attempts left or 3?" Work out your Civil Services age window and remaining attempts from the official CSE rules instead of guessing.

The rules this applies (CSE 2026 notification)

Age is counted as on 1 August of the exam year: you must be at least 21 and under 32 on that date. Relaxations: SC/ST +5 years, OBC +3, PwBD +10 (category relaxations can combine, e.g. OBC + PwBD = 32 + 3 + 10). Attempt caps: General/EWS 6, OBC 9, SC/ST no cap (age limit still applies); PwBD: 9 for GL/EWS/OBC, no cap for SC/ST. An attempt counts only if you actually appear in at least one paper of the Prelims. Every year's notification re-states these against its own 1 August - always confirm against the current year's notification on upsc.gov.in.
