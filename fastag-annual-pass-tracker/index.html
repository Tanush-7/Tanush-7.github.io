How the pass works (current NHAI terms)

One-time Rs 3,075 (revised from Rs 3,000 on 1 April 2026) for one year OR 200 toll plaza crossings, whichever comes first. It works at about 1,150 NHAI fee plazas on National Highways and Expressways - tolls run by states, municipalities or private concessionaires outside the NHAI network still deduct from your FASTag balance. It is for non-commercial vehicles with a valid FASTag, activates within about two hours of payment through the Rajmargyatra app or the NHAI website, and is tied to that vehicle. Terms can change - check the Rajmargyatra app or NHAI's site for the current pass rules and plaza coverage before you buy or rely on it.
