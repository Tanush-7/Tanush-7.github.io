Job Offer Comparator

Two offers and can't decide? Score each on what matters to you, set your own weights, and see the totals.

Before you start

This matrix only organises your ratings - it does not know which offer is better for you. Adjust the weights to what you actually value, score honestly, and treat the totals as a thinking aid, not a verdict. Pay figures you enter are your own numbers (use the in-hand figure from each offer letter); this tool does no tax math.

IdeaMeaning
Weighted scoreEach factor's rating multiplied by its weight, added up. Higher means it fits your stated priorities better - nothing more.
Close scoresIf the totals are within a few points, the matrix is telling you the choice is genuinely close; dig into the specific factors where they differ.
Pay deltaSimple arithmetic on the in-hand numbers you entered, shown per year so the trade-off against other factors is visible.
