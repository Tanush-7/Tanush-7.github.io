# Rent & Utility Tracker

Month-by-month rent, electricity, water and charge tracking for landlords and PG owners.

- **Problem sources**: Reddit requests for a [rental management app covering rent, water bill, electricity bill and payment records](https://www.reddit.com/r/bangalore/comments/10lll07/what_are_some_problems_in_india_that_you_want_a/) and for a [PG management app](https://www.reddit.com/r/developersIndia/comments/1isvhnk/whats_your_i_wish_there_was_an_app_for_that/).
- **Features**: rooms/tenants with monthly rent, per-month sheet (rent, electricity units x rate, water, other), paid/due tracking, collected/pending summary, CSV export.
- **Run it**: open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site.
- **Privacy**: all records stay in this browser (localStorage). A record-keeping aid, not accounting or legal software.
